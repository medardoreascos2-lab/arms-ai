# ARMS AI Phase 4 synthetic disaster recovery runbook

These drills validate sequence evidence only. They do not recover production,
grant operator release, enable execution, or mutate a current store.

## DATABASE_CORRUPTION

1. `ASSERT_SYNTHETIC_SCOPE`
2. `DECLARE_RECOVERY_REQUIRED`
3. `BLOCK_EXECUTION_AND_MUTATIONS`
4. `ISOLATE_CORRUPT_DATABASE`
5. `SELECT_COMPLETED_BACKUP`
6. `RESTORE_TO_ISOLATED_TEST`
7. `VERIFY_CHECKSUMS_SCHEMA_TENANTS_ROWS`
8. `VERIFY_AUDIT_AND_RESEARCH`
9. `RECONCILE_WORKERS_SCHEDULER_OUTBOX`
10. `REQUIRE_OPERATOR_RELEASE`

## MISSING_WORKER

1. `ASSERT_SYNTHETIC_SCOPE`
2. `DECLARE_RECOVERY_REQUIRED`
3. `BLOCK_EXECUTION_AND_MUTATIONS`
4. `STOP_SCHEDULER_CLAIMS`
5. `EXPIRE_OR_RECONCILE_WORKER_LEASES`
6. `START_REPLACEMENT_WORKER_IN_TEST`
7. `VERIFY_QUEUE_AND_OUTBOX`
8. `VERIFY_AGGREGATE_HEALTH`
9. `REQUIRE_OPERATOR_RELEASE`

## PARTIAL_BACKUP

1. `ASSERT_SYNTHETIC_SCOPE`
2. `DECLARE_RECOVERY_REQUIRED`
3. `BLOCK_EXECUTION_AND_MUTATIONS`
4. `QUARANTINE_INCOMPLETE_BACKUP`
5. `SELECT_COMPLETED_BACKUP`
6. `RESTORE_TO_ISOLATED_TEST`
7. `VERIFY_CHECKSUMS_SCHEMA_TENANTS_ROWS`
8. `VERIFY_AUDIT_AND_RESEARCH`
9. `REQUIRE_OPERATOR_RELEASE`

## MIGRATION_FAILURE

1. `ASSERT_SYNTHETIC_SCOPE`
2. `DECLARE_RECOVERY_REQUIRED`
3. `BLOCK_EXECUTION_AND_MUTATIONS`
4. `HALT_MIGRATIONS`
5. `INSPECT_SCHEMA_AND_MIGRATION_HISTORY`
6. `SELECT_COMPLETED_BACKUP`
7. `RESTORE_TO_ISOLATED_TEST`
8. `VERIFY_CHECKSUMS_SCHEMA_TENANTS_ROWS`
9. `VERIFY_AUDIT_AND_RESEARCH`
10. `REQUIRE_OPERATOR_RELEASE`
